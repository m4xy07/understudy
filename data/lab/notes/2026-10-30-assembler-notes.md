# Long-read assembler: Flye or hifiasm

We have not chosen between Flye and hifiasm; Flye is what the pipeline runs today.

## Options

Flye copes with our low-coverage soil samples; hifiasm gives more contiguous assemblies but needs HiFi reads at 20x coverage or more.

Imran is running a hifiasm benchmark on the three deepest samples.
