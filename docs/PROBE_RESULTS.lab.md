## What we do about a miss

A miss is fixed by the approver with "Limit who sees this" on the message. The change applies at once to claims and handovers, and is logged.

## Known and accepted residue

- A claim labelled "Corroborated" can owe that label to an agreeing claim the role cannot see. The value is one the role already sees, so nothing new is revealed beyond "another source agrees".
- The Test page shows "Restricted" per check. That a named check has evidence the role cannot open is needed for the continuation plan.
- A role that reads only one side of a conflict sees neither side until the approver settles it in Review. The count in its stub goes up; the topic is not shown.
- Text in a language or wording the lexicons do not cover is a likely miss at the rule stage. The hard items above are of that kind.
